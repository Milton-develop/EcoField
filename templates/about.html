About EcoField

An ecological intelligence platform for offline-first field data collection, biodiversity management, and research collaboration.

Our Mission

EcoField exists to advance biodiversity research and environmental stewardship by providing a unified digital ecosystem for ecological data collection, collaboration, analysis, and knowledge sharing. Our mission is to empower researchers, educational institutions, conservation organizations, and government agencies with tools that simplify fieldwork, improve data quality, and transform ecological observations into meaningful scientific insights.

At the heart of the platform is EcoField Logger, an offline-first field data collection application that enables reliable ecological surveys in remote and low-connectivity environments. Working seamlessly with the broader EcoField ecosystem, it ensures that field observations become part of a connected, searchable, and scalable biodiversity knowledge base.

EcoField is an independent software platform developed by Moses Milton. While initially developed in collaboration with the University of Ghana's Department of Animal Biology and Conservation Science, the platform is intended to evolve as a multi-institution ecological intelligence platform supporting research, conservation, and biodiversity management across academia and industry.

Platform Features

📦

Offline-First

Collect and store data fully offline. Auto-syncs to the cloud when connectivity is restored.

📊

Biodiversity Analytics

Interactive dashboards with biodiversity metrics including Simpson, Shannon, and Evenness indices.

📸

Photo Capture & Upload

Take photos directly in the field or upload from your device gallery to accompany observations.

🌎

Species Explorer

Browse a structured species database by location and survey type with scientific names and metadata.

👥

Group Management

Organize students into groups with secure logins. Admins can upload rosters and manage access.

📈

Research Repository

Publish and share research work with document uploads for the broader scientific community.
