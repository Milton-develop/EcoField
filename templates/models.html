EcoField AI Models

Intelligent tools that turn field observations into ecological insight from identifying species in photographs to understanding the soundscapes around us.

Models Built for Fieldwork

EcoField is expanding its ecological intelligence with purpose-built AI models that support students, researchers, and conservation teams in the field. These models are designed to work alongside EcoField Logger, helping you capture richer data with less effort and greater confidence.

Available

AI Species Identification

Upload a field photograph and EcoField identifies the likely species and classifies the surrounding habitat. Each result includes confidence scores, supporting notes, and a reminder to verify findings with a qualified expert.

  • Species identification from photos
  • Habitat classification
  • Confidence scores and field notes

Bioacoustics

Automated identification of species from their sounds. Bioacoustics will support acoustic surveys of birds, frogs, insects, and other vocal wildlife, making it easier to monitor biodiversity in dense habitats and at night.

  • Bird call and amphibian recognition
  • Acoustic species richness estimates
  • Passive soundscape monitoring
