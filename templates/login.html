Built as an Ecological Intelligence Platform

EcoField is an ecological intelligence platform that brings together field data collection, biodiversity records, research collaboration, ecological analytics, and conservation knowledge into one connected ecosystem. Whether you're documenting species in the field or analyzing years of research, EcoField provides the tools to transform observations into meaningful environmental insights.

  • Offline-first field data collection with EcoField Logger
  • Centralized biodiversity database and research repository
  • Interactive analytics and ecological insights
  • Species explorer with searchable biodiversity records
  • Collaboration tools for institutions and conservation teams

Who Can Use EcoField?

EcoField is designed for everyone involved in biodiversity research, environmental monitoring, and conservation. From students carrying out field exercises to organizations making national conservation decisions, EcoField adapts to every stage of ecological research.

Universities

Manage student fieldwork, collect standardized observations, and simplify ecological practical sessions.

Researchers

Record biodiversity observations, organize datasets, and collaborate across research projects.

Conservation Organizations

Monitor habitats, track species populations, and support conservation initiatives.

Government Agencies

Access biodiversity data to support environmental planning, policy development, and ecological monitoring.

Environmental Consultants

Collect field evidence and generate reliable datasets for ecological assessments and reports.

Citizen Science

Future support for public biodiversity observations that contribute to national ecological databases.

Powerful Platform Capabilities

EcoField combines intelligent field data collection, biodiversity management, ecological analytics, and institutional collaboration into one connected ecosystem. Whether you're a student, researcher, conservation organization, or government agency, the platform is built to simplify how ecological data is collected, managed, and transformed into meaningful insights.

  • Offline-first field data collection with EcoField Logger
  • Centralized biodiversity database for long-term record keeping
  • Interactive dashboards with biodiversity metrics and ecological analytics
  • Species explorer for searching and discovering biodiversity records
  • Research repository for reports, publications, and field studies
  • Secure collaboration tools for universities and conservation organizations
  • EcoField AI for species identification and habitat identification
