About Developer

Hi, I’m Moses Milton Todoe, the developer behind EcoField Logger. I’m a Biological Sciences student, designer, and aspiring software developer passionate about building digital tools that connect science, technology, and create real world impact.

My work is driven by a fascination with how thoughtful design and intelligent systems can simplify complex problems, especially within science, research, conservation, education, and data collection. Through EcoField Logger, I aim to create a modern field research experience and network that allows students, researchers, and conservation teams to collect, manage, and analyze data efficiently, even in low connectivity environments.

With a background in Animal Biology with Conservation Science and Biochemistry, I approach development with both scientific curiosity and creative thinking. I combine frontend technologies and UI/UX design principles to build experiences that are clean, intuitive, functional, and human centered.

Beyond coding, I’m deeply interested in the future of bioinformatics, digital innovation, and technology driven research solutions across emerging markets. My long-term vision is to bridge science, code, and design to create tools that not only work beautifully, but genuinely improve how people learn, research, and solve problems.

If you're interested in collaborating on research projects, digital innovation initiatives, conservation technology, web solutions, branding, or interdisciplinary ideas that combine science and technology, I’d be happy to connect. I'm always open to meaningful collaborations, learning opportunities, and building solutions that create real world impact. Feel free to reach out whether you have a project in mind, need support bringing an idea to life, or simply want to explore possibilities together.
